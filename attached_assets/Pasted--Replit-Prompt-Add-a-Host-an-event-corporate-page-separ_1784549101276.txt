# Replit Prompt — Add a "Host an event" corporate page (separate from registration)

> Paste the **PROMPT** block into the Replit Agent for the P³ site.
> Key point: this is a **different transaction** from an individual sign-up — it must use its own form and its own pipeline, and take **no payment**.

---

## PROMPT

Add a corporate **"Host an event"** service page and route it completely separately from individual community registration. An individual joining and a company commissioning an event are different transactions — they must not share a form or a data pipeline.

### Routing & navigation
- Keep **"Join the community"** as the site's primary hero CTA — do not let this new page compete with it.
- Add a **secondary** nav link **"Host an event"** (and a footer link) → new route `/host-an-event`.
- Add one tasteful homepage section lower down: heading **"Bring P³ to your team or clients"**, one line, and a button to `/host-an-event`. Keep it clearly secondary to the Join CTA.

### The `/host-an-event` page (premium, B2B tone, on-brand: navy `#0E1B2C`, turquoise `#19C3B0`, off-white `#F4F7FB`, Inter, P³ mark)
Sections:
1. **Hero:** "Host a P³ event" + subline: *"We produce the padel event — you take the credit. Team days, client entertainment, launches and offsites."*
2. **What it is:** short paragraph — we design and run the whole thing: format (Americano and others), a hosted MC, live scoring and leaderboard, branding, kit, drinks and the photos.
3. **Formats:** brief cards — Team Day, Client Entertainment, Product Launch / Activation, Conference Social.
4. **What's included + upsells:** what every event includes, plus optional extras (branded shirts, photography/video, custom branding, catering & bar, prizes, an "Exchange" speaker slot).
5. **Why P³:** 3 short trust points (a proven format, a produced experience, a warm way to build relationships — no pitch, no slides).
6. **Enquiry form** (see below) + a **"Book a 15-min call"** option.

### The enquiry form — qualifying, NOT a registration, NO payment
Fields: **Company**, **Contact name**, **Work email**, Phone (optional), **Event type** (dropdown: Team day / Client entertainment / Product launch / Conference social / Other), **Approx. headcount**, **Preferred timeframe or date**, Budget range (optional dropdown), **Message**, and a **GDPR consent** checkbox ("I agree to be contacted about my event enquiry").
- Add a **"Book a call"** button next to/after the form that links to a scheduling tool — use a placeholder `https://cal.com/pcubed` (Cal.com / Calendly) I can swap later.
- On submit: **do NOT take payment**. Store the enquiry in a **separate table/store tagged `type: "corporate-event"`**, send the organiser an email notification, and show a polished confirmation: *"Thanks — we'll be in touch within one working day to scope your event."*

### Data separation (important)
- Corporate enquiries must **not** be added to the member/registration list or the member data hub.
- Keep three separate destinations, tagged at source: `individual` (member hub) · `corporate-event` (enquiries/sales) · `partner` (partnerships). Reuse the site's existing DB/form approach but tag by type.

### Rules
- No payment anywhere on this page.
- Premium, trustworthy B2B feel (a company decision-maker must believe you can deliver) — but same brand system as the rest of the site.
- Mobile-first; accessible labels and focus states; GDPR consent required to submit.
- After building, show me `/host-an-event` and the new homepage section on desktop and mobile.
