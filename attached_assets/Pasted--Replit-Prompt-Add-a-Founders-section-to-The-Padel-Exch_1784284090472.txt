## PROMPT

Add a **"Founders"** section to our existing Padel Exchange landing page. Keep it fully consistent with the site's current design system — do not restyle or break anything already there.

**Placement & nav**
- Add the section to the main landing page with `id="founders"` so it can be linked to as an anchor (`/#founders`).
- Add a "Founders" link to the site's nav/header that scrolls to it. Also add it to the footer nav if one exists.
- Place the section after the "What happens at an event" section and before the final register/CTA section (adjust if that ordering doesn't exist — put it in a sensible spot above the footer).

**Design (match the existing brand)**
- Brand feel: premium, private-members'-club-meets-sports-brand. Deep charcoal/navy background section, one vibrant padel-court accent (turquoise/lime) used sparingly for hovers/dividers, generous white space, bold display heading + clean sans (Inter). Mobile-first.
- Section heading: **"The founders"** with a short intro line underneath: *"Six people who'd rather make the introduction on court than across a boardroom. This is who you're playing with."*
- Layout: a responsive card grid — **1 column on mobile, 2 on tablet, 3 on desktop.** Equal-height cards, comfortable spacing.
- Each card: a **circular photo** at the top, then the founder's **name** (bold), their **role · company** (accent or muted colour, smaller), then a short **bio** (2–3 lines), and an optional **LinkedIn icon link** in the corner that opens in a new tab (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`).
- Photo handling: load from the `founders/` image folder. **If an image is missing or fails to load, show a circular avatar with the founder's initials** on a brand-accent background instead (so it never shows a broken image). Use the initials given in the data below.
- Subtle, tasteful hover: slight lift/shadow on the card and a thin accent underline or ring on the photo. Nothing flashy.
- Accessibility: every photo needs descriptive `alt` text (e.g. "Dev O'Nion"), links must be keyboard-focusable with a visible focus state, and colour contrast must stay AA.
