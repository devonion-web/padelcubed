1. PUBLIC CURRENT-ROUND COURT ASSIGNMENTS
   - Expose the current round's court matchups on a PUBLIC (unauth) endpoint (extend
     GET /events/:id/leaderboard or add GET /events/:id/live): per court — number/name,
     the 4 players by name, teams A/B, current scores; plus round number, roundStartedAt
     and roundDurationMinutes so a client can compute a countdown.
   - Show it on the attendee mobile leaderboard screen: "Round N · Court 1: Alice & Bob
     vs Carol & Dave", so players know where to go without the MC shouting.

2. UNDO / ROLLBACK LAST ROUND (admin)
   - Reopen/roll back the most recently closed round: reverse the point/win deltas and
     restore the previous draw, so an accidental "next round" is recoverable without DB
     surgery. Put a confirm step on it.

3. LATE ARRIVAL — add a player mid-session (admin)
   - Endpoint + mobile control to add a checked-in/walk-in player to a running session;
     they join from the NEXT round's draw. No manual DB inserts.

4. NO-SHOW THIS ROUND (soft sit-out, admin)
   - Mark a player absent for the current/next round only (they sit out) WITHOUT
     removing them; reinstatable. Distinct from DELETE player.

5. BYE FAIRNESS + SCORE SANITY
   - Track who has taken a bye and prefer players with fewer byes when choosing who
     rests on uneven counts, so the same person doesn't sit out repeatedly.
   - Add a sensible score sanity check (reject clearly invalid entries) without blocking
     legitimate scores.

TESTS
   - Public endpoint returns court assignments (unauth) with players + round timing.
   - Undo restores standings + previous draw exactly (points/wins reversed).
   - Late-arrival player appears in the next round's draw.
   - Soft sit-out excludes a player for one round without removing them.
   - Bye distribution stays even across an uneven-count session.
